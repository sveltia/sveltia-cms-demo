We pushed off just before sunrise, when the water was still as glass and the mountains on the far shore were only a darker shade of grey.

For the first hour, nobody spoke. The only sounds were the drip of the paddles and, somewhere in the reeds, a heron complaining about the company.

![Canoe on a calm lake surrounded by forest](/images/canoe.webp)

## What we packed

- A thermos of coffee, which was gone by seven
- Dry bags for the cameras
- Far too many layers, which turned out to be just enough

By the time the sun cleared the ridge, the mist had lifted and the lake was full of colour. We stayed out until lunch and agreed to come back before the season ends.
