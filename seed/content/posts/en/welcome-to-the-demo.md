This is a live demo of [Sveltia CMS](https://sveltiacms.app/). Everything you see is stored in your own browser, so feel free to edit, create and delete anything. Nobody else will see your changes.

## Things to try

- **Edit this post** and watch the preview update as you type.
- **Switch languages** with the locale menu in the editor. This post is available in English, Japanese and French, and you can edit two languages side by side.
- **Translate** a field with one click using the translation button. You’ll need an API key from a translation service.
- **Upload images** to the Asset Library, or drag and drop them into an image field.
- **Search** for entries and assets from the search bar at the top.
- **Open the Field Types collection** to see every built-in field type in action.

## Starting over

The demo content is copied to your browser the first time you sign in. To get it back, open **Settings**, select the **Advanced** tab and click **Erase All Data**.

Ready to use Sveltia CMS on your own site? Check out the [Getting Started guide](https://sveltiacms.app/en/docs/start).
