The trail leaves the forest at about 2,000 metres, and from there it’s nothing but rock, sky and the occasional marmot.

> The mountains are calling and I must go.
> — John Muir

We reached the pass shortly after noon. The wind was strong enough to lean on, so we found a sheltered spot behind a boulder, ate our sandwiches and watched the clouds roll over the peaks.

## Tips for high-altitude hikes

1. Start early. Afternoon storms build quickly in summer.
2. Drink more water than you think you need.
3. Turn back if the weather changes. The summit will still be there next year.
