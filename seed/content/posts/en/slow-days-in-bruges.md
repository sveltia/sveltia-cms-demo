Bruges is best explored without a plan. Every canal seems to lead to another bridge, and every bridge to a café you’ll want to sit in for an hour.

![Wooden café table by a large window](/images/cafe.webp)

We spent three days here and did almost nothing on purpose:

- Took the morning boat tour before the crowds arrived
- Climbed the 366 steps of the Belfry, slowly
- Ate waffles at least once a day, for research

If you go, stay a night or two. The city empties out in the evening, and the quiet streets by the water are the best part.
