You hear the waterfall long before you see it. The trail follows the stream through mossy boulders and fallen trees, getting louder with every bend.

| Distance | Elevation gain | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7.4 km | 420 m | 3 hours |

The last stretch is a scramble over wet rocks, so good boots are a must. At the base of the falls, the spray is cold even in July, and the rocks glow green with moss.

![Misty valley with a river and forested hills](/images/misty-valley.webp)

On the way back, we took the longer loop through the valley, which is flatter and much quieter.
